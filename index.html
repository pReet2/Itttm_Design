Mission & Vision

Mission

To protect India's maritime interests and uphold maritime order in consonance with international laws. To safeguard our coastline, islands, and offshore assets against any threats.

Vision

To be a combat-ready, credible, cohesive, and future-proof force that protects our national interests in the maritime domain and promotes peace and stability in the region.

Charter of Duties

  • Protect India's maritime borders and coastal regions
  • Ensure safe passage for merchant shipping
  • Conduct search and rescue operations
  • Provide humanitarian assistance and disaster relief

How do I access KMS resources?

Technical manuals are available in the KMS section under "Technical Documentation". You need appropriate clearance levels to access specific documents.
