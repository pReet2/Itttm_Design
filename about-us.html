About ITTTM

The Integrated Trial Testing and Tuning Module stands at the forefront of India's naval innovation, ensuring excellence in maritime defense through rigorous testing and cutting-edge technology.

Our Mission

To provide world-class integrated testing, trial, and tuning services for naval vessels, ensuring operational readiness and technological superiority through rigorous quality assurance and innovative testing methodologies that meet the highest standards of maritime excellence.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the premier naval testing facility in the Indo-Pacific region, pioneering advanced testing techniques and fostering self-reliance in defense technology while maintaining unwavering commitment to national security and maritime sovereignty.

Our Values

Excellence in execution, integrity in operations, innovation in approach, and dedication to national defense. We uphold the highest standards of professional ethics while fostering a culture of continuous improvement and technological advancement.

Charter of Duties

Core Responsibilities

  • Conduct comprehensive sea trials for all naval vessels
  • Perform integrated testing of weapons systems and sensors
  • Execute propulsion and power system validation
  • Assess combat readiness and operational capabilities
  • Validate navigation and communication systems

Quality Assurance

  • Ensure compliance with naval quality standards
  • Implement rigorous testing protocols and procedures
  • Maintain detailed documentation and test reports
  • Conduct failure analysis and corrective actions
  • Certify vessel readiness for operational deployment

Technical Support

  • Provide technical expertise to naval personnel
  • Conduct training programs for ship crews
  • Offer troubleshooting and diagnostic services
  • Recommend system improvements and upgrades
  • Coordinate with shipyards and OEMs

Innovation & Research

  • Develop advanced testing methodologies
  • Research emerging naval technologies
  • Implement AI and automation in testing processes
  • Collaborate with defense R&D organizations
  • Drive indigenization initiatives
